## Ideas credited (no text copied)

These projects gave 14All an idea. The wording in 14All is its own, so no license obligation follows; they are named as a courtesy and so a reader can look at the source. All were read from shallow clones on 2026-10-29.

- ponytail (DietrichGebert, MIT): a floor of things never cut for brevity, looking for an existing helper before writing one, and checking the transcript to prove what a benchmark run loaded.
- everything-claude-code (affaan-m, MIT): refusing to bypass git hooks with a tokenizer-based check, and asking before a lint configuration is weakened.
- agent-skills (addyosmani, MIT): the restartable-session list, and a reviewer that never gets the author's conclusion.
- planning-with-files (OthmanAdi, MIT): the plan on disk, the hook that loads it again, and the warning that a silent hook looks the same as no plan.
- learn-harness-engineering (walkinglabs, MIT): evidence for every finished step, one active step, stop conditions, and the verified-versus-unverified split of a handoff note.
- claude-mem (thedotmack, Apache-2.0): "tried and why it failed" in a handoff, and `<private>` text never copied into a note.
- wshobson/agents (MIT) and ruflo (ruvnet, MIT): the tighten-only idea for guard rules and framing injected text as data.
- claude-skills (alirezarezvani, MIT): the regression gate that fails on a dropped case.
- graphify (Graphify-Labs, Apache-2.0): reporting the lines actually read, and labelling a claim read, inferred or unverified.
- tradingagents (TauricResearch, Apache-2.0): saying "not provided" for a missing input, an inconclusive result instead of a default, and invalidating saved progress when the plan changes.
- rtk (rtk-ai, Apache-2.0; its `engine/` and `proxy/` are BUSL-1.1 and were not read for ideas): the four rules for trimming tool output (explicit detail is never trimmed, the same format, never lose the failure, leave a way back).
- headroom (headroomlabs-ai, Apache-2.0): keep a pointer when you cut, keep negations, and do not shorten warnings.
- context-mode (mksglu, Elastic-2.0, an idea only and nothing else taken): printing the answer instead of the data, and choosing a tool by what you will do with its output.
- Understand-Anything (Egonex-AI, MIT): the change-impact method of `impact-check` (changed files, their users, the blast radius), without the graph.
- career-ops (career-ops-hq, MIT): treating text from outside as data, never as instructions, and stopping before an irreversible step.
- wshobson/agents (MIT) and agent-skills (addyosmani, MIT), again: the hypothesis-testing debugger, the one-dimension reviewer with one finding format, the pre-ship checklist and the security auditor's threat-model-first method.

- ui-ux-pro-max (nextlevelbuilder, MIT; its datasets are not used): the priority order of what to check in a UI and the pre-delivery checklist, in the web UI reference.
- taste-skill (Leonxlnx, MIT; its site library and example images are not used): rendering the page at several widths, judging the picture, a capped fix loop, and checking the production build.
- learn-harness-engineering (walkinglabs, MIT), again: the `AGENTS.md` template layout and the idea of `check:harness`.
- everything-claude-code (affaan-m, MIT), again: the invisible-character and personal-path checks.
- agent-skills (addyosmani, MIT), again: the done criteria and the rollout note, and the routing evaluation.
